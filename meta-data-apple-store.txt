APP NAME
Quietforge

SUBTITLE
On-device writing studio

KEYWORDS
writing,draft,offline,prompt,workshop,copy,lesson,caption,journal,rewrite,studio,notes,private

PROMOTION
File a recipe, spark a draft on this phone, then rewrite it — tighten, expand, warmer, formal, shorter. Keep what you would send. No account, no Wi-Fi.

DESCRIPTION
Quietforge is a private writing studio that lives on your phone.

A workshop is one craft you write for — shop windows, classroom openers, a weekly letter. Give it a code, pick a craft, and set how many recipes you are aiming for.

A recipe is the job: a title, a seed of facts, a form (caption, hook, letter, lesson, product, poem and more), optional tags for voice and audience, and a mood picture if it helps.

Spark composes on the device. It fills the form from your seed and tags, then you apply rewrite moves — Tighten, Expand, Warmer, Formal, Shorter. Grade the draft: Shelve, Rough, Tuned, or Ready. Kept drafts are saved on the recipe.

There is no cloud model and no network call. Quietforge is a studio and a local composer, not a chatbot.

WHAT YOU GET

• Workshops grouped by craft — newsletter, product, classroom, fiction, social, personal, speech
• Recipes with a seed, a form, tags and an optional mood photo
• An on-device composer with twelve forms and five rewrite moves
• Spark sessions that queue seeds first, or only the recipes you pinned to the hearth
• Five draft stages — Seed, Rough, Tuned, Ready, Shelved — with a keep-rate at the end of every session
• A hearth for the pieces you would actually send
• Ledger with draft-stage spread, breakdown by craft and by form, and total sparks
• Move log recording every time a recipe is filed under another workshop
• Search across titles, seeds, tags and forms
• Paper daylight, lamp light, or match the phone
• Export everything to a single JSON file and restore it whenever you want

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

There is no account and no sign-in. Quietforge makes no network calls at all — workshops, recipes, photos and spark history live in this app's own storage on your phone. Camera and photo library access are optional and used only when you attach a picture to a recipe.

Good for shop owners writing a window card, teachers drafting a lesson opener, and anyone who wants a draft without handing the brief to a server.
