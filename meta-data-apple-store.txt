APP NAME
Kettleleaf

SUBTITLE
Tea notes, fully offline

KEYWORDS
tea,tasting,oolong,green,puerh,cupping,steep,caddy,leaves,aroma,journal,offline,private

PROMOTION
File a leaf, cup it on this phone, then rewrite the note. Keep what you’d steep again. No account, no Wi-Fi.

DESCRIPTION
Kettleleaf is a private tea journal that lives on your phone.

A caddy is one family you drink — morning greens, evening oolongs, a travel tin. Give it a code, pick a family, and set how many leaves you are aiming for.

A leaf is the cup: a name, a steeping seed, a form (aroma, liquor, body, origin, blend, steep, pairing and more), optional tags, and a photo of the dry leaf if it helps.

Cupping writes on the device. It fills the form from your seed, then you rewrite. Grade the steep: Flat, First, Settled, or Cellared. Kept notes are saved on the leaf.

There is no cloud model and no network call.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account and no sign-in. Kettleleaf makes no network calls. Camera and photo library are optional and used only when you attach a picture to a leaf.

Good for people who steep at the desk, pack a travel tin, or want tasting notes without handing the garden to a server.
